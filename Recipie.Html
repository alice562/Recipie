SIMPLE OMELEETE RECIPIE

An easy and quick dish,perfect for any meal.This classic Omeleet combines beaten
eggs cooked to perfection,optionally,filled with your choice of cheese,vegetables or meat.

Preparation Time

  • Total:Approximately 10 minutes
  • preparation:5 minutes
  • cooking:5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • salt,to taste
  • pepper,to taste
  • 1 table spoon of butter or oil
  • optional fillings:diced vegetables,cooked meat,hearbs

    • Instructions

    1. Beat the eggs
      2. In a bowl; beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper untill they are well mixed.
      You can add a teaspoon of water or milk for a fluffler texture.
    2. Heat the pan:
      3. Place a non-sticky frying pan over medium heat and add butter or oil.
    3. Cook the Omelette:
      4. Once the butter is melted and bubbling,pour in the eggs. Tilt the pan to ensure the eggs evenly coat the surface.
    4. Add the fillings(optional)
      5. When the eggs beggin to set at the edges but still slightly runny in the middle,sprinkle your choosen fillings over one half of the omlette.
    5. Fold and serve
      6. As the omelette countinues to cook, carefully lift one side and fold it over the fillings. Let it cook it cook for another minute,then slid it on to a plate.
    6. Enjoy:
      7. Serve hot,with additional salt or pepper if needed.
